LocalRouter

Jarvis on your own computer. The models run on your Spark; the tools, your history and every permission stay here.

1. Your Spark

On the Spark, make a one-time code (ten minutes, single use):

deploy/jarvis/pair.sh add "My Mac" --scopes inference,media,read

Paired with as . The token is kept .

2. Permissions on this Mac

System Settings may still show LocalRouter switched on: that entry belongs to an older build. In the list, select LocalRouter, remove it with −, add it again with +, then quit and reopen LocalRouter. Or reset it here and switch it on again.

Ask only for what you use. A voice call needs the microphone; computer control needs Accessibility (and Screen Recording for apps that hide their windows). macOS asks about Automation the first time Jarvis uses an app.

Anything that could send, buy, delete or post, or type into a field that is not a search box, waits for your approval on screen, confirmed with . Stop any time: ⌘⇧⎋, the menu bar, or move the pointer to the top-left corner.
