        Let Jarvis use this computer
        
          
        
            
        Jarvis works in the background as a virtual mouse: your own mouse and keyboard stay yours, and it does not ask before each step. This is the only time LocalRouter asks. On a Mac, macOS may ask once now for LocalRouter to control System Events and your browser: allow it, and nothing asks again during a task. Stop it any time with ⌘⇧⎋, the menu bar, or the top-left corner.
