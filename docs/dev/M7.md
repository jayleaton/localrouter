# M7: MiniMax H3 text to video + audio, the plan

What ComfyUI 0.37.0 does is in `tools/twin/VIDEO-PORT.md` (file:line references). This file is what LocalRouter builds
and how it is checked. Same method as the image tool: a Python twin that runs every op on a kernel LocalRouter owns,
gated against the original; the Zig engine bit-exact with the twin, op by op and end to end.

## The reference ("the video twin")

ComfyUI's MiniMax H3 text-to-video pipeline, op for op (packing, the curve time embedding, per-token modulation, RoPE,
the audio carry, `res_multistep` with eta 0 on the `simple` schedule, the VAEs, the frame and sample conversions), with
these declared substitutions (each one is where ComfyUI's numerics depend on a backend or on history):

| Part | ComfyUI | The twin (and the engine) | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Noise | CPU `mt19937` `randn`, video then audio | LocalRouter's portable generator (`noise.py`), video then audio, same layout | portable across machines; ComfyUI's draw depends on the CPU's vector path |
| DiT linears | bf16 (stock) or tfvideo NVFP4 with *delayed* input scales | TensorFold NVFP4 W4A4 with **static** calibrated input scales; FP8 later | delayed scales make a run depend on the previous forward |
| DiT attention | SDPA (stock) / comfy-kitchen INT8 (tfvideo) / sol-attn on steps 2+ (workflow) | comfy-kitchen INT8 attention, copied as source, every step dense | the shipping speed path; sparse attention is a later speed lever with its own gate |
| q/k norm + RoPE | `comfy_kitchen.rms_rope_split_half_` | the same kernel, copied | already a fixed kernel |
| Block elementwise | bf16 torch ops (stock) / tfvideo Triton | our CUDA kernels with tfvideo's arithmetic (fp32 inside, one rounding) | no Triton in the engine for video |
| fp32 islands (patch projections, curve modulation, final heads), refiner, text attention | torch fp32 / bf16 / SDPA | our deterministic fp32 and bf16 GEMMs and attention | cuBLAS / SDPA picks kernels by shape |
| Text encoder | NVFP4 AWQ weights dequantized to fp32, cuBLAS SGEMM, fp32 SDPA | the same dequantization, our fp32 GEMM and attention, fp32 throughout | as above |
| Video VAE | fp16 ViT (stock) or int8 convrot (workflow), tiled | the fp16 path on our fp16 GEMM and attention, the same tiling and blends | the fp16 file is the unquantized one |
| Audio VAE | fp32 BigVGAN on cuDNN (TF32 allowed for convolutions) | fp32 on our kernels, no TF32 | TF32 is a backend choice |
| Sampler scalars | CUDA `log` / `expm1` / `exp` on 0-d tensors | portable f64 math rounded to f32, shared by twin and engine | portable |
| mp4 | PyAV, libx264 defaults, AAC defaults | ffmpeg; the gate compares decoded frames (uint8) and audio samples before encoding | encoders are not bit-reproducible across builds |

Gates against ComfyUI (the twin is "the same model"): per-step velocity cosine of the twin's DiT vs stock ComfyUI's
bf16 DiT on the same inputs, against the floor two ComfyUI attention backends give each other; text encoder output
cosine vs ComfyUI's; VAE PSNR vs ComfyUI's decode of the same latents; audio SNR; and whole clips scored as tfvideo
did (LPIPS, DINO, log-mel) at 768x448, 2 s.

Gates of the engine against the twin: per op alone and chained (`localrouter check h3-replay`), then whole clips: the uint8
frames and the fp32 audio samples byte-equal, on sm_120; speed no slower than the twin.

## Order of work

| Step | Content | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M7a | kernels: INT8 attention and rms_rope (copied from comfy-kitchen 0.2.35), block elementwise, fp32 GEMM, NVFP4 dequant; the twin's DiT (`stk_twin/h3/`), calibration, captures | kernel tests; twin DiT vs ComfyUI per step within the floor ratio; one step captured |
| M7b | Zig DiT and replay | step bit-exact alone and chained |
| M7c | text encoder (twin + Zig) | twin vs ComfyUI cosine; Zig bit-exact |
| M7d | sampler, video VAE, audio VAE (twin + Zig), mp4 | VAE gates; Zig bit-exact |
| M7e | the `minimax_h3` tool behind `/v1/videos`; end to end | frames and audio byte-equal to the twin; timing |

Sizes: gates and captures at 768x448, 2 s (56 frames: 17 latent frames x 336 patches + 66 audio rows + text); timing
at 1344x768, 5 s. Pods: PRO 6000 for anything that holds the bf16 DiT (40 GB) beside ComfyUI, the PRO 4500 otherwise.

## Status

**V1** (a rented PRO 6000, 19 min, $0.66): stock ComfyUI 0.37.0 headless on the pod (`stk_twin.comfy_run`):
768x448, 56 frames, 20 steps, bf16 DiT: 27.3 s cold, 18.2 s warm; with `--use-ck-attention` 17.1 s warm; Turbo LoRA 8
steps 8.0 s warm. Output h264 768x448 24 fps 56 frames + AAC stereo 32 kHz. Weights on the volume
(`stk/models/minimax-h3`, 61 GB).

**V2** (a rented PRO 6000, 12 min, $0.42):

- H3 kernels vs ComfyUI's / tfvideo's formulas (`h3.test_ops`, 20 checks): data moves exact, arithmetic within one bf16
  ulp of the terms, fp32 GEMM within the fp32 accumulation bound, deterministic. All pass.
- comfy-kitchen copies vs the installed 0.2.35 wheel (`h3.test_kitchen`): INT8 attention and rms_rope **bitwise equal**
  and deterministic at every H3 shape (S 80 / 4096 / 9000, head dim 128 and 64, packed and contiguous, with and without
  the K shift).
- Structure gate (`h3.gate`, 768x448, 56 frames, 8-step schedule, twin bf16 vs ComfyUI's bf16 DiT on SDPA; floor =
  ComfyUI with another SDPA backend):

  | | refined text context | video rel L2 / floor, per step | audio rel L2 / floor, per step |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | twin, bf16 attention | cos 0.99999 | 0.56 - 1.19 | 0.81 - 1.57 (one step over 1.5, the rest <= 1.24) |
  | twin, INT8 attention | cos 0.99999 | 0.87 - 2.52 | 1.82 - 2.90 |

  The bf16 twin sits at ComfyUI's own backend-to-backend distance apart from one audio step (1.57x; 186 audio rows
  against 8,000 video rows, so its rel L2 is the noisiest number in the table). INT8 attention costs about 2x the floor,
  its quantization; that, like NVFP4, is judged on whole clips (LPIPS / DINO / log-mel), not by the floor ratio.

**Offline since the hold (written and type-checked, `zig build test` green, nothing run on a GPU yet):**

- Twin: the 32B text encoder (`h3/te32.py`), the audio VAE (`h3/vae_audio.py`, fp32 BigVGAN, `--fmad=false`), the
  video VAE (`h3/vae_video.py`, fp16 ViT3D on our fp16 tensor-core GEMM, ComfyUI's clip and tile schedule), the end to
  end generator (`h3/generate.py`), their GPU tests and capture entry points; the pack carries `tokenizer.json`.
- Zig: the DiT step, the sampler (bit-equal plans, host test), the text encoder (inv_freq bit-equal, host test), both

- Gates, each replaying a twin capture alone and chained: `localrouter check h3-te`, `h3-replay` (one DiT step), `h3-avae`,
  `h3-vvae` (plus the frame hash), `h3-e2e` (ids, every step's state, latents, frame and waveform hashes).
